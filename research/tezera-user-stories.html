User stories

Version 1 · 8 October 2026 · based on the discovery surveys and PRD version 3 · SDGP Agile Team Project

These stories describe what organisers, vendors, freelancers, consumers and admins need from Tezera. Each one comes from something people told us in the surveys, links to the PRD requirements that deliver it, and has acceptance criteria the team can test against.

How to read a story

  • Format. "As a [role], I want [something], so that [reason]", then acceptance criteria. A story is done when every criterion passes.
  • Priority comes from the PRD, using MoSCoW: Must Should Could, and Later for anything after the ATP release, such as ticketing.
  • PRD lists the requirements that deliver the story. Click one to jump to it.
  • R1 · PRD change means the story needs a change the PRD doesn't have yet. The R number links to that recommendation in the survey analysis. Until the group accepts it, treat its priority as proposed.
  • Evidence is the survey finding behind the story. Numbers are counts of respondents, out of 26 attendees, 7 organisers or 13 vendors and freelancers.
  • Roles. Like the PRD, these stories say "consumer" for someone who attends events, and "provider" for vendors and freelancers together.
Role
Priority

Consumers

People who go to events. They want every detail in one place, live news when plans change, and help finding their way and their friends on the day.

US-C01

Find every event in one place

Must

As a consumer, I want to browse and search upcoming events by keyword, category, date, district and price, so that I stop missing events that were only announced on a page I don't follow.

Acceptance criteria

  • Search matches event titles, organisers and line-ups.
  • Filters for category, date range, district and price, including free events, can be combined.
  • Each result shows the date, venue, price or 'free', and the organiser with a Verified badge where earned.
  • Past and cancelled events are left out of results by default.
PRD EV-05Evidence: 17 of 26 attendees have missed events because they found out too late; scattered information was the most common complaint.
US-C02

Hear about new events from organisers I follow

Should

As a consumer, I want to follow organisers and be notified when they publish an event, so that I hear about it the day it's announced, not after tickets sell out.

Acceptance criteria

  • A Follow button on every organiser profile and event page.
  • A push and in-app notification when a followed organiser publishes an event.
  • This notification type can be switched off in settings.
PRD EV-04, MS-08Evidence: "I wasn't following that page"; "I find out about the event on the day itself" (attendees).
US-C03

See the essentials at a glance

Must

As a consumer, I want the date, time, venue, price, line-up and organiser at the top of the event page, so that I can decide whether to go without hunting through posters and group chats.

Acceptance criteria

  • The top of the page shows date and time, venue name and address, price or "free", line-up or schedule, and organiser.
  • Where tickets are sold elsewhere, the page links to the official seller (proposed).
  • Everything the organiser has published about the event is on this one page.
PRD EV-01Evidence: Attendees decide on date and time (24 of 26), price (23), venue (21) and line-up (18).
US-C04

Know what to wear, bring and expect

Should

As a consumer, I want a "Know before you go" section with dress code, parking and transport, what to bring, rules, food and drink prices, and rain cover, so that I turn up prepared, and don't have to message the organiser with the same questions as everyone else.

Acceptance criteria

  • Organisers can fill in each field; empty fields are hidden.
  • Rules can include an age limit, re-entry and banned items.
  • Food and drink can show a price range in LKR.
  • Changes to this section notify people who marked the event Going (proposed).
PRD EV-01 R5 · PRD changeEvidence: In open answers, 5 attendees asked about dress code and 3 about transport or parking; others asked about prices, re-entry and umbrellas.
US-C05

Tell a real event from a fake one

Must

As a consumer, I want to see whether an event's organiser has been verified, and to report an event that looks fake, so that I don't pay a scammer or travel to an event that isn't happening.

Acceptance criteria

  • Verified organisers carry a Verified badge on their profile, their events and their posts.
  • Events by unverified organisers are labelled as such.
  • A Report button on every event page sends it to the admin report queue.
PRD AC-08, CM-04, AD-03Evidence: 24 of 26 attendees trust information from the official organiser account; one described duplicate ticket sellers.
US-C06

Be told when plans change

Must

As a consumer, I want to be notified straight away if an event I marked is moved, postponed or cancelled, so that I don't turn up on the wrong day or wait for a refund announcement that never comes.

Acceptance criteria

  • A change to date, time or venue, a postponement or a cancellation notifies everyone who marked the event 'Going' or 'Interested', by push and in the app.
  • The event page shows a banner saying what changed and when.
  • A cancelled event stays visible, marked Cancelled, rather than disappearing.
PRD EV-02, MS-05Evidence: "Two events … got cancelled last moment and then … there was no update regarding a reschedule or refund" (attendee).
US-C07

Know if the event is running late

Should

As a consumer, I want to see a live status (on time, running late with a new start time, postponed, cancelled), so that I don't arrive hours early or miss the start of something that was delayed.

Acceptance criteria

  • The organiser can set the status from the app in two taps.
  • Running late requires a new estimated start time.
  • A status change is shown on the event page with the time it was set, and pushed to people who marked 'Going'.
PRD EV-02, MS-05 R6 · PRD changeEvidence: Delays and last-minute changes were the most common on-the-day complaint (6 attendees).
US-C08

Ask the organiser and see earlier answers

Must

As a consumer, I want to ask questions on the event page and read the answers to other people's questions, so that I don't depend on helpline numbers that don't answer, or a WhatsApp group I joined too late to read.

Acceptance criteria

  • The event Q&A shows every question with the organiser's answer, visible to everyone.
  • Someone who arrives late sees all earlier answers.
  • Questions that are personal go to a private chat with the organiser.
PRD MS-06, MS-01Evidence: "I was added to the WhatsApp group late and I couldn't view the previous messages" (attendee). Another found helpline numbers unanswered.
US-C09

See which friends are going

Must

As a consumer, I want to see which of my friends marked an event as Going, so that I can decide whether to go and make plans with them.

Acceptance criteria

  • The event page shows friends who marked Going, if their privacy settings allow it.
  • Going status is private by default (proposed).
  • I can mark Going or Interested from the event page in one tap.
PRD CM-05, CM-06, EV-06Evidence: 20 of 26 attendees say who else is attending matters, more than the line-up.
US-C10

Plan with people going to the same event

Must

As a consumer, I want to join the event group chat and the event board, so that my group can agree where and when to meet, and I can find people to go with when my friends can't.

Acceptance criteria

  • Anyone who marked 'Going' can join the event group chat.
  • The event board allows meetup threads such as 'Meet at Gate 2'.
  • Block and report are available in both.
PRD MS-04, CM-01Evidence: WhatsApp groups are the main way attendees coordinate (14 of 26); one asked for a way to find people to go with.
US-C11

Find toilets, entrances and parking at the venue

Must

As a consumer, I want to open the venue map from the event page and find facilities by their pins, so that I don't wander a large venue looking for the toilets or the right entrance.

Acceptance criteria

  • The map opens from the event page and supports zoom and pan.
  • Standard pins cover stages, stalls, entrances and exits, toilets and first aid.
  • Standard pins also cover parking, registration or ticket counter, seating, food and drink, and water (proposed).
  • Tapping a pin shows its label; the map always shows the latest version.
PRD VM-02, VM-04 R7 · PRD changeEvidence: 18 of 26 have struggled to find something: toilets (13), entrances (10), seating (8), parking (8), registration (6).
US-C12

Use the map when there is no signal

Could

As a consumer, I want the venue map, times and latest announcements to stay available offline once I have opened them, so that I can still find my way when the crowd takes out mobile data.

Acceptance criteria

  • Opening an event page online saves its map, schedule and announcements on the phone.
  • Offline, the saved copy opens with a "last updated" time.
  • The saved copy refreshes when the phone is back online.
PRD VM-04 R8 · PRD changeEvidence: "Mobile reception is often very poor … not a single app is loading because of the lack of network" (attendee).
US-C13

See which stalls will be there

Must

As a consumer, I want to see the list of stall vendors, what they sell and where their stall is, so that I know what food and drink to expect and can find the stall I want.

Acceptance criteria

  • Once stalls are confirmed, the event page lists each vendor with its stall number and category.
  • Each stall vendor links to its profile and to its pin on the map.
  • Vendors can show a price range (proposed).
PRD EV-01, HR-12, VM-02Evidence: Attendees asked about food prices (2) and described stalls running out (3).
US-C14

Review an event I went to

Must

As a consumer, I want to rate the organiser and the event after attending, so that other people can judge the organiser by how past events went.

Acceptance criteria

  • Unlocked by a 'Going' mark made before the event started; opens when it ends and closes 14 days later (proposed).
  • Categories: organisation, value for money and overall experience (proposed).
  • An added category for facilities: toilets, queues and food availability (proposed).
  • One review per person per event.
PRD RV-01, RV-02 R11 · PRD changeEvidence: 6 of 26 trust reviews from previous attendees; washrooms, queues and stalls running out were common complaints.
US-C15

Share an event with my friends

Must

As a consumer, I want to share an event to an Instagram story or a WhatsApp group with a link that shows a preview, so that my friends see the details without installing anything first.

Acceptance criteria

  • A Share button on every event page and post.
  • The link shows the cover image, title, date and venue as a preview.
  • The link opens the event in the app, or on the web portal without logging in.
  • A "share to story" image sized for Instagram stories.
PRD EV-04 R9 · PRD changeEvidence: 25 of 26 attendees find events on Instagram and 22 through friends; WhatsApp groups are where plans are made.
US-C16

Get my money back when an event is cancelled

Later

As a consumer, I want a full refund when an event I bought a ticket for is cancelled, so that I'm not left out of pocket by an organiser who disappears.

Acceptance criteria

  • Arrives with ticketing, after the ATP.
  • A full refund on cancellation or admin unpublishing (TK-11).
PRD TK-11Evidence: Two cancelled events with "no update regarding a reschedule or refund" (attendee).
US-C17

Get in quickly, with the ticket I want

Later

As a consumer, I want to choose a ticket tier and get in with a QR code, so that I don't queue at a ticket counter and can pay for the experience I want.

Acceptance criteria

  • Arrives with ticketing, after the ATP (TK-01, TK-06, TK-07).
PRD TK-01, TK-07Evidence: Attendees named ticket-counter queues, slow check-in and "proper ticket tiers".

Organisers

People and companies who run events. They want to hire providers they can trust, run stalls without spreadsheets, and keep attendees informed.

US-O01

Find providers outside my network

Must

As an organiser, I want to search for vendors and freelancers by category, district, rating and verified status, so that I'm not limited to the few people I already know when they're busy or not right for the job.

Acceptance criteria

  • Filters for category, district, rating, rank and Verified can be combined.
  • Results are ordered by rank score.
  • Each result shows category, districts, indicative prices, rating and badges.
PRD HR-01Evidence: All 7 organisers rely on personal contacts and past vendors; finding someone suitable is their top difficulty (6 of 7).
US-O02

Judge a provider before hiring them

Must

As an organiser, I want to see a provider's portfolio, completed jobs and reviews from other organisers, so that I can trust someone new as much as a personal recommendation.

Acceptance criteria

  • The profile shows the portfolio, the number of completed jobs and the tier badge.
  • The review page shows the average rating, the average for each category (quality, punctuality, communication, value for money) and every review.
  • Every review comes from a completed booking.
PRD RV-03, AC-04, RV-05Evidence: 6 of 7 rate feedback from other organisers 5 out of 5 for usefulness; they judge by recommendations (6), portfolio (5) and experience (5).
US-O03

See reviews for the kind of job I need

Could

As an organiser, I want to filter a provider's reviews by the service and type of event, so that I know how they handled a job like mine, not just jobs in general.

Acceptance criteria

  • Each review is tagged with the service and the event type from its booking.
  • The review page can be filtered by service and event type.
PRD RV-03 R10 · PRD changeEvidence: "If we can filter out for what kind of work he got feedback for, i think thats enough of a 'testimonial'" (organiser).
US-O04

Only ask providers who are free

Should

As an organiser, I want to filter providers by the date of my event, so that I don't waste time contacting people who are already booked.

Acceptance criteria

  • Providers mark dates as booked or unavailable on a calendar.
  • Search can filter by an available date.
PRD HR-02Evidence: Availability is a deciding factor for 5 of 7 organisers; 3 struggle to find someone available.
US-O05

Send a complete brief

Must

As an organiser, I want a booking request and job post form that asks for everything a provider needs to quote, so that I get accurate quotes the first time and fewer surprises on the day.

Acceptance criteria

  • Required: event, date, start and end times, location, service and budget.
  • Optional: expected audience, list of deliverables, run-of-show timings, equipment provided, and rules (proposed).
  • Files such as a brief or mood board can be attached.
PRD HR-03, HR-05 R1 · PRD changeEvidence: Missing details are providers' most common complaint; 3 of 7 organisers find getting and comparing quotes hard.
US-O06

Compare quotes side by side

Must

As an organiser, I want to see every quote on a job post next to the provider's rating and message, so that I can choose on price and quality together.

Acceptance criteria

  • Quotes are listed with price, rating, tier badge and message.
  • I can accept one or more quotes; each acceptance creates a booking.
  • Providers who were not chosen are notified.
PRD HR-06, HR-07Evidence: Comparing prices (3 of 7) and unclear pricing (3 of 7) are hiring difficulties.
US-O07

Agree payment terms in writing

Must

As an organiser, I want to set the deposit, instalments, due dates and cancellation terms on each booking, so that both sides know what was agreed, and I can keep track of many small payments.

Acceptance criteria

  • A booking records service, date, price, deposit, each instalment with its amount and due date, and cancellation terms.
  • The provider sees these terms before accepting.
  • Changing the terms after confirmation needs both sides to accept (proposed).
PRD HR-04, HR-08 R2 · PRD changeEvidence: "Most payments are made as fractions and keeping up with the deadlines … is hard and time consuming" (organiser).
US-O08

Replace a provider who cancels

Should

As an organiser, I want to reopen a cancelled booking as a job post in one step, so that I can find a replacement before it is too late.

Acceptance criteria

  • When a provider cancels, I am notified with their reason.
  • One action turns the cancelled booking into a job post with the brief already filled in.
  • A cancellation within 7 days of the event (proposed) shows on the provider's profile.
PRD HR-08, HR-05 R4 · PRD changeEvidence: "When we realise this, it's too late to switch to another person" (organiser).
US-O09

Run stall applications in one place

Must

As an organiser, I want to open stall slots, review applications, invite vendors and give out stall numbers in the app, so that I stop managing stalls through phone calls and spreadsheets.

Acceptance criteria

  • Stall slots have a type, fee and application deadline.
  • Applications can be approved or rejected; vendors can also be invited directly.
  • Each approved vendor gets a stall number, which appears on the event page and the map.
  • The stall fee is shown in the app and paid outside it.
PRD HR-10, HR-11, HR-12, HR-13Evidence: 6 of 7 organisers manage stalls by contacting vendors directly; 2 use spreadsheets.
US-O10

Share the venue layout before the event

Must

As an organiser, I want to upload my floor plan and pin the stages, stalls, entrances and facilities, so that attendees can find their way without relying only on signs and staff.

Acceptance criteria

  • Upload a PNG or JPG layout per event.
  • Add, move, relabel and remove pins, then republish.
  • Attendees always see the latest version.
PRD VM-01, VM-02, VM-03Evidence: All 7 organisers coordinate the venue layout, but only 1 shares a digital map.
US-O11

Promote an event and get it shared

Must

As an organiser, I want to post images and reels linked to my event, and have them easy to share outside the app, so that my promotion reaches people on Instagram and WhatsApp, not only inside Tezera.

Acceptance criteria

  • Posts and reels can link to an event page.
  • Posts and event pages have share links with previews (see US-C15).
PRD EV-03, EV-04Evidence: Instagram is the main channel for 6 of 7 organisers; getting people to engage and share is their most common promotion difficulty (3).
US-O12

Update everyone at once

Must

As an organiser, I want to send an announcement and set the event's live status from my phone, so that attendees hear about changes from me, not from rumours in group chats.

Acceptance criteria

  • Announcements reach everyone who marked 'Going' or 'Interested' and my followers.
  • Announcements appear on the event page as well as in notifications.
  • Live status (US-C07) can be set from the same screen.
PRD MS-05, EV-02Evidence: 2 of 7 organisers struggle to keep information up to date; attendees want live updates.
US-O13

See how much interest an event has

Must

As an organiser, I want to see 'Going' and 'Interested' counts, page views and post engagement, so that I can tell whether promotion is working before the day.

Acceptance criteria

  • 'Going' and 'Interested' counts update as they change.
  • Analytics show page views, post and reel engagement, and 'Going' marks over time.
PRD OD-01, OD-04Evidence: Organisers struggle with reaching enough people (2 of 7) and measuring results (1).
US-O14

Track each event's hiring and payments

Must

As an organiser, I want one view of requests sent, quotes received, confirmed bookings, stall approvals and payments due, so that nothing slips through when I'm coordinating many vendors at once.

Acceptance criteria

  • The dashboard lists every request, quote, booking and stall for an event, with its status.
  • Bookings show the next payment due and whether the provider has confirmed receiving earlier ones (proposed).
PRD OD-03 R3 · PRD changeEvidence: Talking to vendors is the most time-consuming task for 5 of 7 organisers.

Vendors and freelancers

Businesses and individuals hired for events: photographers, DJs, caterers, decorators and others. They want new work, complete briefs, and fewer jobs that are cancelled late or paid late.

US-P01

Be found by organisers I don't know

Must

As a provider, I want a profile that organisers find when they search my category and district, so that my work stops depending only on referrals.

Acceptance criteria

  • The profile shows category, services, indicative prices in LKR and districts served.
  • Verified providers show a badge after admin approval.
  • Top providers appear on category and district leaderboards.
PRD AC-03, HR-01, RV-06Evidence: 11 of 13 get work through referrals and 11 through past clients; several want to reach a wider audience.
US-P02

Show my work

Must

As a provider, I want a portfolio of photos and videos from past events on my profile, so that organisers can judge my quality before they contact me.

Acceptance criteria

  • Upload photos and videos within size limits.
  • Each item can name the event or type of event.
  • Completed jobs on Tezera are counted on the profile automatically.
PRD AC-04Evidence: Providers show their work through social media (9 of 13) and portfolios (6); one has no portfolio yet.
US-P03

Get the full brief before I quote

Must

As a provider, I want every booking request and job post to state the deliverables, duration, audience, timings and equipment provided, so that I can quote accurately and avoid unpaid extra work on the day.

Acceptance criteria

  • Requests show the required fields from US-O05 and any optional ones the organiser filled in.
  • I can ask questions in the booking chat before quoting.
  • The brief is kept on the confirmed booking.
PRD HR-03, HR-05, MS-02 R1 · PRD changeEvidence: All 13 need date and location; deliverables, run-of-show and audience details are the most often missing.
US-P04

Check an organiser before accepting

Must

As a provider, I want to see an organiser's verification, past events and reviews from other providers, so that I don't work for someone who has a history of not paying.

Acceptance criteria

  • The organiser profile shows the Verified badge, past events and reviews from providers (payment, communication, planning).
  • It shows the share of bookings the organiser paid on time (proposed, from US-P07).
PRD AC-02, AC-08, RV-03Evidence: Providers want to know an organiser's previous events (8 of 13) and reviews (6) before accepting.
US-P05

See payment and cancellation terms before I accept

Must

As a provider, I want the deposit, instalments, due dates and cancellation terms on every request, so that I can decide whether the job is worth the risk.

Acceptance criteria

  • Terms set by the organiser (US-O07) are shown before I accept, decline or quote.
  • I can propose different terms with my quote.
  • The agreed terms are stored on the booking.
PRD HR-04, HR-08 R2 · PRD changeEvidence: Payment terms are the first thing 11 of 13 providers want to know; 4 want the cancellation policy.
US-P06

Reply with my own quote

Must

As a provider, I want to answer a booking request with a different price, so that I can negotiate in the app with a record of what was offered.

Acceptance criteria

  • I can accept, decline or reply with a quote.
  • The organiser accepts or declines the quote; acceptance creates the booking.
PRD HR-04Evidence: Negotiating prices is a difficulty for 6 of 13.
US-P07

Record payments received

Should

As a provider, I want to mark each instalment as received, and have late ones flagged, so that organisers who pay late or not at all build a visible record.

Acceptance criteria

  • Each instalment on a booking has a due date and status: due, received or overdue.
  • I mark an instalment as received; the organiser is reminded on its due date (proposed).
  • The organiser's profile shows the share of bookings paid on time, counting only bookings with terms.
PRD HR-08 R3 · PRD changeEvidence: 7 of 13 face unpaid or late payment; one waited 3 months and paid costs from personal funds.
US-P08

Find job posts that fit me

Must

As a provider, I want to browse job posts that match my category and district, and apply with a quote, so that I can find work beyond the people I already know.

Acceptance criteria

  • Job posts are filtered to my category and district by default.
  • I apply with a quote and a message before the deadline.
PRD HR-06Evidence: Finding clients and suitable jobs is hard for some (2 each); one wants "a simpler way to find reliable event opportunities".
US-P09

Keep track of my bookings

Should

As a provider, I want a calendar of my bookings with reminders for events and payment due dates, so that I don't double-book or forget a deadline.

Acceptance criteria

  • Confirmed bookings appear on my availability calendar automatically.
  • Reminders before each event and each payment due date; each type can be switched off.
PRD HR-02, MS-08Evidence: "Have a better planning and managing system. To track when things are due and to give reminders of deadlines" (provider).
US-P10

Be protected from last-minute cancellations

Should

As a provider, I want cancellations to need a reason and late ones to show on the organiser's record, so that organisers think twice before cancelling a confirmed job.

Acceptance criteria

  • Cancelling a confirmed booking requires a reason.
  • A cancellation within 7 days of the event (proposed) shows on the profile of whoever cancelled.
  • A cancelled booking unlocks a review of the organiser (proposed).
PRD HR-08 R4 · PRD changeEvidence: Last-minute cancellation is the top difficulty for providers (9 of 13).
US-P11

Review the organiser

Must

As a provider, I want to rate an organiser on payment, communication and planning after a job, so that other providers know who pays and who plans well.

Acceptance criteria

  • Unlocked by a completed booking or a stall at an event that has ended.
  • Categories: payment, communication, planning.
PRD RV-01, RV-02Evidence: "The organizer of the concert fled the venue, without paying the artists, the vendors or any other event expense" (provider).
US-P12

Be reviewed fairly

Must

As a provider, I want reviews of me to come only from real jobs, with a right to reply and a way to dispute them, so that one unfair review doesn't damage my business.

Acceptance criteria

  • Only organisers with a completed booking can review me.
  • I can post one public reply to each review (proposed).
  • I can report a review; admins can hide reviews that break the rules, and disputes use the booking record.
PRD RV-01, RV-07, AD-04 R10 · PRD changeEvidence: Providers asked for two-way reviews tied to completed jobs, a range of good and bad reviews, and dispute resolution.
US-P13

Apply for a stall

Must

As a provider, I want as a vendor, to see open stall slots with their fees and apply, so that I can get stalls at events without knowing the organiser personally.

Acceptance criteria

  • Open stall slots show the stall type, fee and deadline.
  • I can apply, and accept or decline invitations; approval gives me a stall number.
  • Vendors only; freelancers do not take stalls.
PRD HR-10, HR-11, HR-13Evidence: Organisers fill stalls by contacting vendors directly (6 of 7); 7 of 13 providers ask about the payment or stall fee before accepting.

Admins

Platform staff. They verify accounts, remove fake events, settle disputes and moderate reviews.

US-A01

Verify organisers and providers

Must

As an admin, I want to check verification documents and approve or reject accounts with a reason, so that users can trust the Verified badge.

Acceptance criteria

  • Documents are visible to admins only.
  • Organisers must be verified before they open stalls, post jobs or send booking requests.
  • Every decision is logged.
PRD AC-08, AC-09, AD-01Evidence: Trust in the organiser was the top concern for attendees (24 of 26) and providers alike.
US-A02

Take down fake events

Must

As an admin, I want to unpublish an event reported as fake, so that no one pays a stall fee or travels to an event that isn't real.

Acceptance criteria

  • Unpublishing closes the event's stall applications and job posts.
  • People who marked 'Going' or 'Interested' are told the event was removed.
PRD AD-03Evidence: Attendees described duplicate ticket sellers; a provider asked for "a system to show the credibility of an event".
US-A03

Settle disputes using the booking record

Must

As an admin, I want the agreed terms, payments recorded and chat history attached to each dispute, so that I can judge non-payment and no-show cases on evidence.

Acceptance criteria

  • A dispute shows the booking, its payment terms and status, and the chat.
  • Outcomes: keep or hide related reviews, warn, suspend or ban.
PRD AD-04Evidence: Providers described late payment, verbal confirmations followed by cancellation, and an organiser who left without paying.
US-A04

Keep reviews honest

Must

As an admin, I want to hide reviews that break the rules and leave fake accounts out of ratings, so that rankings reflect real work.

Acceptance criteria

  • Reported reviews enter the report queue.
  • Hidden reviews and fake accounts are left out of ratings and rank scores.
PRD RV-07, AD-02Evidence: Providers asked for "unbiased reviews" and "honest and fair feedback from both".
