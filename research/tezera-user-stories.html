User stories

Version 3 · 15 October 2026 · scenarios in Given / When / Then · based on the discovery surveys and PRD version 5 · SDGP Agile Team Project

These stories describe what organisers, vendors, freelancers, consumers and admins need from Tezera. Each one comes from something people told us in the surveys, links to the PRD requirements that deliver it, and has Given / When / Then scenarios the team can test against.

  • Priority comes from the PRD, using MoSCoW: Must Should Could, and Later for anything after the ATP release, such as ticketing.
  • PRD lists the requirements that deliver the story. Click one to jump to it.
  • PRD version 5. The group accepted all twelve recommendations from the survey analysis (R1–R12) on 15 October 2026, so the stories that depended on them now link to the new or extended requirements. The same version added an interactive 2D map (VM-08 to VM-15). See what changed in version 3.
  • Evidence is the survey finding behind the story. Numbers are counts of respondents, out of 26 attendees, 7 organisers or 13 vendors and freelancers.
  • Roles. Like the PRD, these stories say "consumer" for someone who attends events, and "provider" for vendors and freelancers together.

Consumers

People who go to events. They want every detail in one place, live news when plans change, and help finding their way and their friends on the day.

US-C01

Find every event in one place

Must

Scenarios

Search by keyword

Given
a published event has the band "Wildfire" in its line-up
When
I search for "Wildfire"
Then
the event appears in the results
And

Combine filters

Given
there are upcoming events in several categories, districts and prices
When

Then

Read a result

When
I look at any search result
Then

And
a Verified badge appears next to verified organisers

Hide past and cancelled events

Given

When
I search without changing the default filters
Then
neither event appears in the results
PRD EV-05Evidence: 17 of 26 attendees have missed events because they found out too late; scattered information was the most common complaint.

What changed in version 3

All twelve survey recommendations were accepted on 15 October 2026 and added to PRD version 5. The 16 stories that waited on them now link to the new or extended requirements: R1 (US-O05, US-P03: HR-03, HR-05 extended), R2 (US-O07, US-P05: HR-04, HR-08 extended), R3 (US-O14, US-P07: new HR-15), R4 (US-O08, US-P10: new HR-16), R5 (US-C04: new EV-08), R6 (US-C07: new EV-09), R7 (US-C11: VM-02 extended), R8 (US-C12: new VM-15, mobile app only), R9 (US-C15: EV-04 extended), R10 (US-O03, US-P12: new RV-08, RV-09), R11 (US-C14: new RV-10). R12 settled the age question: new story US-C20 (EV-10). The minimum account age of 16 (proposed), with a parent or guardian's agreement for under-18s (AC-01), has no story here because sign-up has none yet.

PRD version 5 also added an interactive 2D map: zones, stall listings with items and prices, search by item, 'Find on map', stage line-ups and a stall-type filter. New stories US-C18, US-C19, US-O15 and US-P14 cover it.
