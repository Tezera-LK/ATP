# SDG Alignment — [Product Name]

**Project:** [Product Name], an event organisation platform for Sri Lanka (SDGP)
**Date:** 30 September 2026

## Summary

[Product Name] aligns most closely with **SDG 8: Decent Work and Economic Growth**, with **SDG 9: Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure** as a secondary goal. The link is the hiring marketplace: it helps freelancers and small vendors find paid work at events and build a visible track record.

## Primary goal: SDG 8 — Decent Work and Economic Growth

| Target | What the target covers | How the platform contributes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 8.3 | Entrepreneurship, creativity and the growth of micro, small and medium-sized enterprises | Freelancers (photographers, videographers, DJs, MCs) and small vendors (F&B stalls, small agencies) find paid work through direct bookings, job posts and stall applications. Reviews, rankings and portfolios give them a track record that helps them win more work. |
| 8.9 | Sustainable tourism that creates jobs and promotes local culture and products | Concerts, festivals and exhibitions create jobs and showcase local culture and products. The platform makes these events easier to organise and promote. |

## Secondary goal: SDG 9 — Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure

| Target | What the target covers | How the platform contributes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 9.3 | Bringing small enterprises into value chains and markets | Small vendors join events through a digital marketplace instead of relying only on personal contacts. |

## Weaker links (use only with a specific feature to point to)

| Goal | Target | Link |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SDG 10 — Reduced Inequalities | 10.2 (social and economic inclusion) | Sinhala and Tamil language support. Individual freelancers can compete with agencies on reviews rather than connections. |
| SDG 11 — Sustainable Cities and Communities | 11.4 (cultural heritage) | Only if the platform focuses on cultural or heritage events. |

## How we will measure the contribution

- Number of providers (vendors and freelancers) who get at least one paid booking through the platform
- Bookings per provider over time
- Number of stall vendors who take part in events through the platform
