Eventline

A mobile app and web portal where Sri Lankan event organisers hire vendors and freelancers, and attendees find events and each other. Every finished job leaves a review the next organiser can read.

The problem2

Running one event means juggling five separate channels.

Organisers promote, sell, hire and update attendees in different places. None of it builds a shared record of who did good work.

Promote Instagram and Facebook pages
Sell tickets A separate ticketing site
Hire vendors and freelancers Word of mouth and personal contacts
Share the venue layout Rarely shared before the day
Update attendees WhatsApp groups and posts

Nothing carries over from one event to the next. A photographer who does good work at a festival has no record of it that the next organiser can check.

Who feels it

Organisers

Find vendors and freelancers through word of mouth, with little trustworthy reputation data.

Vendors

Hear about events and open stall slots only through personal contacts.

Freelancers

Photographers, DJs and MCs struggle to compete with agencies without portfolios and reviews in one place.

Attendees

Event details are spread across social pages, venue layouts are rarely shared and there is no easy way to find others going.

The solution4

Promotion, hiring, community and venue maps in one app.

Eventline is a mobile app and web portal with four modules. Organisers are at the centre. They publish events and hire the vendors and freelancers who work at them.

Events and promotion

For organisers and attendees

  • Event pages with date, venue, line-up and schedule
  • A promotion feed of posts and reels from organisers
  • Search by category, date and district; follow organisers

Hiring marketplace

For organisers, vendors and freelancers

  • Direct booking requests, or job posts answered with quotes
  • Stall slots filled by invitation or open application
  • Verified-only reviews, rank scores, badges and leaderboards

Community

For everyone

  • A discussion board for every event, plus city and topic communities
  • Role badges beside every name
  • In-app chat, announcements and a public Q&A on each event

Venue maps

For organisers and attendees

  • Organisers upload the floor plan and drop labelled pins
  • Stages, stalls, entrances, toilets and first aid
  • Attendees open it from the event page and zoom in

Ticketing and entry

Ticket tiers, reserved seats, free RSVPs and QR entry at the gate are out of scope for the ATP. They are the first item beyond the ATP (page 8).

How the modules feed each other

Events create demandEach new event needs photographers, DJs, stalls and sound.
Jobs create reviewsCompleted bookings unlock reviews on both sides.
Reviews guide hiringThe next organiser hires on track record, not contacts.
Community builds audienceEvent boards keep people talking until the next event.
Key workflows5

Two routes to a hire, one route to a stall.

Organisers can hire in two ways. Both end in the same confirmed booking, and reviews open only when the organiser marks the job complete.

Hiring a vendor or freelancer

Filling stalls and publishing the venue map

Vendor appliesOr accepts the invitation from the organiser.
Organiser approvesAnd each approved vendor gets a stall number.

SDG alignment7

More paid work for freelancers and small vendors.

Eventline’s clearest link is to SDG 8. Through the hiring marketplace, freelancers and small vendors find paid work at events and collect reviews they can show future clients.

8Decent work and economic growth

Primary goal

Decent work and economic growth

8.3

Growth of micro and small enterprises. Photographers, DJs, MCs and small vendors win paid work through bookings, job posts and stall applications. Reviews and portfolios help them win the next job.

8.9

Tourism that creates jobs and promotes local culture. Concerts, festivals and exhibitions create jobs and showcase local culture and products. Eventline makes them easier to organise and promote.

9Industry, innovation and infrastructure

Secondary goal
