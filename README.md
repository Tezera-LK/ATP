# Eventline

A mobile app and web portal where Sri Lankan event organisers hire vendors and freelancers, and attendees find events and each other. Every finished job leaves a review the next organiser can read.

**Second-year Agile Team Project (SDGP)** · Project proposal, September 2026

## Roadmap (from the proposal)

**During the ATP**

1. **Discovery:** interview event organisers, survey vendors, freelancers and attendees, and confirm the name and backlog.
2. **ATP release,** a mobile app and web portal with:
    - accounts and verification
    - events and promotion feed
    - hiring marketplace and stalls
    - reviews and ranking
    - community and chat
    - venue maps
    - admin and moderation
    - Sinhala and Tamil, if time allows
3. **Live pilot:** one or two partner organisers and the vendors they already use run a real event through Eventline, producing the first reviews and SDG measures.

**Decision point:** after the pilot, the group decides whether to take Eventline further.

**Beyond the ATP**

1. **First — ticketing and entry:** ticket tiers and reserved seats, free RSVP events, QR tickets and gate scanning, refunds and cancellations, and attendee reviews of events.
2. **After that — payments in the app:** hiring payments and deposits, stall fees, ticket and hiring commission, and venue map extras.
3. **After that — hiring for private events:** weddings, birthdays and other private events.
4. **Later version — premium accounts:** VIP and fast-sale ticket access.

## How the proposal and the PRD differ

- **Name:** the PRD uses "[Product Name]" because it was written before the name was chosen. The proposal uses Eventline.
- **Ticketing:** the PRD's MVP includes ticketing and entry, with ticket payments in the app. The proposal puts ticketing and entry outside the ATP, as the first item after it, together with refunds and attendee reviews of events.
- **Revenue:** the PRD starts with a ticket commission in the MVP. The proposal lists ticket and hiring commission under "payments in the app", after the ATP.
- **Discovery and pilot:** the proposal schedules a discovery phase (interviews and surveys) at the start of the ATP and a live pilot at the end. The PRD mentions interviews and surveys only as proposed checks, and the pilot only as a proposal and an open question.
- **Private events:** the proposal plans hiring for private events after the ATP. In the PRD, this is still an open question.
- **Wording:** the proposal calls consumers "attendees".
